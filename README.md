# Water Tank Level Indicator

A simple water tank level indicator circuit made and simulated using Tinkercad.

## Idea

The main idea is to use water as part of the electrical path.
When the water reaches a certain level, it completes the circuit and turns on the corresponding LED.

Transistors are used as switches to control the LEDs.

## Components

* Transistors
* Resistors
* LEDs
* Wires
* Water level probes

## Simulation

The circuit was designed and tested using Tinkercad.
